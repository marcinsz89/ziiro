Nintendo platform logos

The logos identify the consoles listed in Ziiro's compatibility browser.
Nintendo owns the marks. Wikimedia Commons describes these particular files
as public domain due to the copyright originality threshold and flags
separate trademark rights. Ziiro is an independent project.

The bundled PNGs are resized from these files. The Nintendo 3DS wordmark is
cropped to 3DS with transparent padding. Both Switch marks are rendered in red.
